About The Game

Ramen Hustle was a game made for a Group project I did in RMIT. The game features the protagonist John Hustler, as they work to pay off their debt by working in the titular Ramen Shop. Players serve ramen and clean bowls of ramen to various customers.

My role in this project was the main Artist.
I contributed through the animations, models and visual effects in the game.

The Capybara | John Hustler

John Hustler is the main character in Ramen Hustle. The concept was focusing on a capybara who owed a debt to a gang.The game uses 2D character sprites within a 3D environment in Unity. The sprites are animated frame by frame and use cel-shading. The shading uses a wave pattern for lighting and each character in this style is limited to a few colours.

The Visual Style

All the sprites in the game tried to follow this style guide. The outlines used follows the colours of the character and followed the simple hand-drawn look from the reference. The shading took inspiration from the reference and had a wavy pattern separating the highlight, midtone and shadow.

The Boss Customer

I did the animation for the Boss customer - who appears during certain days in the game and requires extra bowls serve to. It has an animation for shouting for more bowls of Ramen. Additionally, the boss uses an idle and a walk animation.

Additional NPC Characters

Ramen

In the game, there were 3 types of ramen; meat, seafood and vegetables and had 3 tiers associated with them. Each tier represented a higher level of prestige over the other and the visual effects in-game represent that.

Tech Art - Ramen Bowl Visual Effects

The ramen served by the player are 3D models and I worked on creating over the top visual effects to distinctly represent which type of ramen the player was serving. This allowed the player to feel their ramen was getting better as they got more upgrades.
