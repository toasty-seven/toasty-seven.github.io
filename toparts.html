Top Arts

About

This was a 3D animation done using Blender in 2022 for VCE Studio Arts. The animation features a cyberpunk sci-fi environment that takes elements from Melbourne's city center and reimagines them into a different setting. I leveraged use of photo textures and 3D scanning to replicate the city's environment.

Exhibition

My animation was featured in the National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne as part of the Top Arts 2023 exhibition. Inside the gallery, it was projected onto the wall where the animation would play.

Additionally, stills of my artwork were used for the promotional material for the exhibition - it was mainly featured for posters as well as website and other promotional material.

Progress Development

The environment was constructed in Blender and mainly used 3D models with realistic photo textures and a realistic rendering workflow to get a gritty look. It uses many 3D scans and photo textures of buildings from Melbourne to capture the realistic look to the animation. Additional short renders were made for the background advertisements that play throughout the short animation.

The main inspiration for the visual style was from Ian Hubert and his Dynamo Dream series of short films
