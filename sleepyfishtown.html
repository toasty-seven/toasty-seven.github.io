About the project

This was a Unity project for an RMIT University elective. This project focused on 3D modeling and shader work to create a comic-book style environment. The environment centers upon a sushi truck operated by a robotic chef that comedically is controlled by a fish.

the Concept

The world is set in a sci-fi, robot-inhabited city with inspiration being taken from games like Overwatch and Marvel Rivals. The main draw would be the Sushi food truck, operated by another robot chef. The robots are largely inspired by the single-purpose robots in WALL-E and that this one robot fish would have the job of making sushi.

3D Model gallery

The 3D models make followed being low-poly and hand painted in Blender. The models were painted using Blender's texture paint mode - using blocky shapes and half-tone brushes helped me get the comic-look I was aiming for.

I used stencil textures premade in Photoshop to get further detail in the texturing - with the corner/edge wear and grime especially proved useful.
