About the game

ROAM follows Nuggets – a small, feathered Sinosauropteryx prima – on a heartfelt journey to find its elusive mate’s nest. Watch as Nuggets travels through a beautifully stylised Cretaceous birch forest, painted in hues of gold and amber.

Help Nuggets explore an environment where they will jump, crouch and weave through dense forest, feast on fruits and insects and encounter other dinosaurs along the way

I worked as the main Artist and Game Designer on this project - I handled the 3D modeling, rigging and texturing of characters, did the 2D artwork including the Key art and programmed the interactions in the game in Unity using C#.

The Sinosauropteryx | Nuggets

The protagonist in the game is Nuggets, a Sinosauropteryx prima that inhabits the Cretaceous forest. Nuggets was modelled, rigged and hand-textured in Blender to achieve a illustrated and stylised look. The model was created with a low-poly target in mind and to get detail out of alpha cards and textures.

NPC Characters

These characters appear throughout the game for the player to find and interact with. As with all models in the game they were modelled, textured and rigged in Blender and set up in Unity. The dinosaurs were chosen to fill out different niches in the environment, including having a piscivore in the river area and herbivores in the forest plains.

Microraptor

These small bird dinosaurs were commonplace in the game. They would sit around on the ground and hop and fly away when the player got close. This was done to make the player feel like they were in the environment and interacted with the characters in the scene.
