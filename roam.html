About the game

ROAM follows Nuggets – a small, feathered Sinosauropteryx prima – on a heartfelt journey to find its elusive mate’s nest. Watch as Nuggets travels through a beautifully stylised Cretaceous birch forest, painted in hues of gold and amber.

Help Nuggets explore an environment where they will jump, crouch and weave through dense forest, feast on fruits and insects and encounter other dinosaurs along the way

I worked as the main Artist and Game Designer on this project - I handled the 3D modeling, rigging and texturing of characters, did the 2D artwork including the Key art and programmed the interactions in the game in Unity using C#.

The Sinosauropteryx | Nuggets

The protagonist in the game is Nuggets, a Sinosauropteryx prima that inhabits the Cretaceous forest. Nuggets was modelled, rigged and hand-textured in Blender to achieve a illustrated and stylised look. The model was created with a low-poly target in mind and to get detail out of alpha cards and textures.

Coding interactions.

I was the programmer and did the implementations of the interactables in the game. The main scripts I worked on add in collectables, jumping & dragging and adding cutscenes to the game. Additionally I added in the sound effects and music functionality through scripting.

Coding Microraptor

These small bird dinosaurs were commonplace in the game. They would sit around on the ground and hop and fly away when the player got close. This was done to make the player feel like they were in the environment and interacted with the characters in the scene.

These were made interactable with the player through scripting. Through the void OnTriggerEnter, it would run a coroutine for the bird to hop then fly.

The coroutine begins with hopping away from the player using Quaternion rotation = Quaternion.LookRotation(awayDir, Vector3.up); to rotate it away from the player then a loop: for (int i = 0; i < hopCount; i++) to hop away from the player through a lerp and sine wave.

Jumping

In the game, the player can jump in certain places to go over obstacles. It uses the Jumping script to jump the player to jump point GameObject locations.

The jump script runs the coroutine JumpPlayer with player input. This first disables the character controller to stop the player from moving whilst jumping.

Then the script lerps between the start position and end jump point with a parabola for the jump motion. The switch(jumpType) is used to change the jumpState which changes with jumping animation the player uses: one for jumping up and the other for falling down. Then after landing it reenables movement and plays a dust visual effect and sound.

Interactables

There were two interactable scripts in the scene - cutscene and collectables. This cutscene script runs a coroutine that plays a timeline when the player is interacting or entering the trigger of the script. This plays an animation, vfx and sound - letting the player destroy obstacles & interact with the environment.

Another type of interactable is the collectable - it has a certain health amount and uses the same input to interact. After interacting it adds to the counter to show how many collectables the player has gotten.

The interactable script uses function TryHit whenever there is player input on a collectable object. This then reduces health and then runs the coroutine SpawnReplacementAfterDelay to instantiate destroyed variant of the GameObject and turns off the UI and collectable.

NPC Models

These characters appear throughout the game for the player to find and interact with. As with all models in the game they were modelled, textured and rigged in Blender and set up in Unity. The dinosaurs were chosen to fill out different niches in the environment, including having a piscivore in the river area and herbivores in the forest plains.
